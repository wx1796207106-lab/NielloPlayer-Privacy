Who we are

NielloPlayer (“the app”) is an offline music player for Android, provided by the independent developer of NielloPlayer.

We are the controller of the processing described in this policy. You can reach us at the email address published under “Developer contact information” on the Google Play listing; any request about this policy or your information can be sent there.

What this policy covers

This policy explains which information the app processes on your device, for what purposes, and what choices you have. It applies to the Android version.

This policy is provided online only, with no copy stored in the app: what you open inside the app is the current version, never a copy that grew stale alongside an installer.

It does not cover what happens after you hand a file to another app. From that moment the receiving app’s own policy applies — see “Sharing”.

What we do not ask for

The app has no account and requires no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or postal address, and we do not collect your contacts, messages or call logs, photos or videos, calendar, location, or advertising identifier.

There are no ads in the app, and no third-party SDK for analytics, social login or behavioural tracking is integrated. We do not build user profiles, do not make targeted recommendations from your behaviour, and make no automated decisions.

Information the app processes on your device

Library and playback state. To play music, the app reads audio files stored on your device. The library index, playback queue, playback position, audio-quality and equaliser settings, and the links between songs and lyrics are kept in the app’s private storage so your previous state is restored next time. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Folders you choose. Folders outside the system media library — a music folder on an SD card, for example — can only be reached through the Android system file picker. The app can read only the folder you personally selected; it cannot read other locations and does not request broad access to all files. Lyrics files you add yourself are authorised through the same picker.

Receive folder. Files received over Bluetooth, the local network or the web page are written to the folder you choose in Settings → Library → Receive folder, which defaults to Music/NielloPlayer in shared device storage. What ends up in that folder is yours to manage — see “Sharing”.

Permissions the app uses

Each permission takes effect only when the matching feature is used. We do not ask in advance for something you are not using yet, and refusing an optional permission never takes away basic playback.

Audio files. Android 13 and later use READ_MEDIA_AUDIO; Android 10 to 12 use READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE. Both are used to scan and play your music. WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE is declared only for Android 10 and below, to remember the folder you selected. You can revoke these at any time in system settings; afterwards the app shows only what it has already indexed.

Network. INTERNET serves four things: hosting a small local web page for transferring songs, discovering the other device on the same local network, talking to Google Play when you choose to tip, and loading the online page for this policy. The app runs no servers of its own and never uploads your library, lyrics or settings.

Wi-Fi and hotspot state. ACCESS_WIFI_STATE and CHANGE_WIFI_STATE are used to read whether Wi-Fi is connected and whether a hotspot is running, so the app can tell you which address to open and what to do. On some Android versions the system requires CHANGE_WIFI_STATE even to query hotspot state; there it is only a precondition for reading. The app never turns Wi-Fi or the hotspot on or off, only reuses the one you enabled in system settings, and has no ability to share mobile data with other devices.

Bluetooth. BLUETOOTH_CONNECT is used for headphone playback (A2DP) and to list paired devices. BLUETOOTH_SCAN is declared to the system as not being used to derive location (neverForLocation) and is used to find nearby transfer targets inside the app. BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE lets the other device discover this one while you have the receive page open — for at most 300 seconds, after which the system returns to not-discoverable and the app cannot extend it. Android 11 and below have no such split permissions; the equivalent abilities come from BLUETOOTH and BLUETOOTH_ADMIN granted at install time, and the app uses them only for Bluetooth headphone playback and song transfer.

On Android 11 and below the system ties Bluetooth scanning to location permission. The app declares ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION only for those versions, and asks inside the app once, only when you open Bluetooth discovery or the receive page. You may refuse; after refusing you can still send to already-paired devices. The app never reads, stores or uploads any location value.

Foreground service and notifications. FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK keep music playing in the background; POST_NOTIFICATIONS shows playback controls on the lock screen and in the notification shade.

Billing. com.android.vending.BILLING is required for the optional tipping provided by Google Play.

Optional tips

Tipping is entirely voluntary and handled entirely by Google Play Billing.

Google acts as an independent controller and receives and processes your payment information under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We never receive, view or store your card number, billing address or Google account at any point, and purchase records never enter the app.

The only local record related to tipping is a flag meaning “there is a tip we have not yet thanked you for in person”, used to show the matching thank-you note the next time you open the tipping page. It contains no amount and no payment credential, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Tipping unlocks no features. Everything works without paying.

Diagnostic information

Because the app is distributed through Google Play, Google collects app-quality data at platform level — Android vitals: crashes and stack traces, application-not-responding (ANR) events, start-up time, battery and memory use, permission grants and denials, together with the app version, Android version, device model and country/region at the time. This data is collected by Google Play directly, requires no code inside the app, and does not pass through us. It is used only to assess and improve app quality; we see aggregated results in Play Console and never an individual user’s record.

That collection is carried out by Google as an independent controller and is governed by Google’s own privacy policy. You can turn off sharing in Settings → Google → Usage & diagnostics; afterwards Google stops collecting this data from your device.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not share it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

There are two situations in which files leave for another app, and you start both:

Bluetooth transfer. The app opens the Android system share sheet and you choose the receiving app. Once the file has been handed over, its storage, copying and further distribution are governed by that app’s policy and fall outside this one.

Receive folder. The default Music/NielloPlayer sits in shared device storage, where the system lets other apps read its contents. If you move the receive folder elsewhere, what is readable is decided by Android’s storage rules and the permissions you granted — a choice you make.

Apart from Google Play Billing and the Android vitals described above, we share your information with no third party, and there is no situation in which your library information needs to be transferred abroad. Your library, lyrics, playback history and settings stay on your device and have never been transmitted anywhere.

Security

The app’s private storage is protected by the Android app sandbox; other apps cannot read it.

We run no back-end service that receives your data, and playback does not depend on the network, so your library and settings cannot be read through any server of ours.

Data produced by links you open in your own browser is handled by that browser.

The online page for this policy is hosted on GitHub Pages and opened inside the app as the system browser’s custom tab: the address bar is rendered by the browser, so you can confirm that you really are on arcanesparkle.github.io and not on some in-app shell. GitHub, as host, receives the technical information needed to deliver the page, such as your IP address and the time of the request; the browser may also keep its own record and cache of that visit according to its settings, and the display language you pick on the page is stored by the browser too. All of this happens between the browser and the host: the app does not read the page content and cannot control what the browser retains.

Retention and deletion

As long as you keep the app, your information stays on your device.

Uninstalling the app deletes the library index, playback queue and all settings in its private storage. We keep no copy — there is no cloud data for you to request deletion of separately.

The app has no separate “clear library” command. At present the way to rebuild the index from scratch is to uninstall and reinstall.

The playback state needed for the interface and for background playback lives only in memory and is released when you leave the app.

Your rights

Depending on the law where you live, you may have the right to access the information we hold about you, to have inaccurate information corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to processing, and to receive it in a portable form.

Because your information exists only on your device and we hold no copy, these rights can be exercised directly:

  • Access and correction: view and change your library, folders and all settings inside the app.
  • Deletion: uninstalling the app deletes all local data.
  • Withdrawing consent: revoke any granted permission at any time in system settings; the matching feature stops working and the rest is unaffected.

If you believe our processing infringes data protection law, you have the right to complain to a supervisory authority in the place where you live, work, or where the alleged infringement occurred. You are also welcome to contact us first; we will do our best to resolve it directly.

Legal bases for processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we process information on the following bases:

  • Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)): reading your audio in order to play it, remembering your playback position and audio settings, and receiving files into the folder you specify. This processing is necessary to provide the service you asked for.
  • Your consent (Article 6(1)(a)): optional permissions such as Bluetooth discovery. You may withdraw it at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

We do not rely on “legitimate interests” for advertising, profiling or behavioural analysis, because the app does none of those things.

Children and personal data

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Since the app collects no personal information from anyone, parents need to make no extra arrangements for a child using it.

No sale, no sharing

Under the definitions of “sell” and “share” in the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (CCPA/CPRA), we do not sell and do not share personal information. The app carries no advertising and integrates no analytics SDK for advertising or profiling; we derive no commercial benefit from your information. This does not change over time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the last-updated date above.

If a change affects how information is processed, we will explain it inside the app or on the store listing before it takes effect, rather than only changing the date.

Contact us

For anything about this policy or your information, contact us at the developer email published under “Developer contact information” on the Google Play listing.

我们是谁

NielloPlayer（下称「本应用」）是一款 Android 平台的离线音乐播放器，由 NielloPlayer 的独立开发者提供。

本政策涵盖什么

它不涵盖你把文件交给其它应用之后发生的事情。从那一刻起，适用的是接收方应用自己的政策，详见「信息的共享」。

我们不索取的信息

本应用不设账号，无需注册或登录。我们不索取你的姓名、邮箱地址、电话号码或通讯地址，也不收集你的联系人、短信与通话记录、照片与视频、日历、位置或广告标识符。

应用在你的设备上处理的信息

曲库与播放状态。 为了播放音乐，应用会读取你设备上存储的音频文件。曲库索引、播放队列、播放位置、音质与均衡器设置，以及歌曲与歌词的对应关系，保存在应用私有目录中，以便下次打开时恢复你此前的状态。这些信息留在你的设备上，不会发送给我们。

你选择的文件夹。 系统媒体库之外的文件夹，例如 SD 卡上的音乐文件夹，只能通过 Android 系统文件选择器访问。应用只能读取你亲自选中的那个文件夹，读不到其它位置，也不申请全盘文件访问权限。你自行添加的歌词文件同样经由这个选择器授权。

接收目录。 通过蓝牙、局域网或网页收到的文件，写入你在「设置 → 曲库 → 接收文件夹」中指定的目录，默认是设备公共存储中的 Music/NielloPlayer。该目录的内容由你自行管理，详见「信息的共享」。

应用使用的权限

每一项权限都只在对应功能被使用时才起作用，我们不会在你还未用到该功能时提前索要，也不会因为你拒绝某项可选权限而限制基础播放功能。

音频文件。 Android 13 及以上使用 READ_MEDIA_AUDIO，Android 10 至 12 使用 READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE，用于扫描并播放你的音乐。WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE 仅对 Android 10 及以下声明，用于保留你选定的文件夹。你可以在系统设置中随时收回这些权限，收回后应用只显示已经索引过的内容。

网络。 INTERNET 用于四件事：在本机提供一个局域网网页（网页传歌）、在同一局域网内发现对端设备、在你选择打赏时与 Google Play 通信、以及加载本政策的在线页面。应用不运营任何自有服务器，也不上传你的曲库、歌词或设置。

Wi-Fi 与热点状态。 ACCESS_WIFI_STATE 与 CHANGE_WIFI_STATE 用于读取 Wi-Fi 是否已连接、热点是否已开启，以便告诉你该访问哪个地址、该给什么提示。部分 Android 版本上，系统要求 CHANGE_WIFI_STATE 才能查询热点状态，这只是读取的前提。应用从不打开或关闭 Wi-Fi 与热点，只复用你自己在系统设置里开启的那个，也不具备把蜂窝数据共享给其它设备的能力。

蓝牙。 BLUETOOTH_CONNECT 用于耳机播放（A2DP）与列出已配对设备。BLUETOOTH_SCAN 已向系统声明不用于推断位置（neverForLocation），用于在应用内发现附近的传送目标。BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE 用于在你打开接收页期间，让对端设备能够发现本机——该状态最长持续 300 秒，到期后由系统自动恢复为不可被发现，应用无法延长。Android 11 及以下没有上述三项细分权限，对应的能力由安装时授予的 BLUETOOTH 与 BLUETOOTH_ADMIN 覆盖，应用仅用于蓝牙耳机播放与传歌。

Android 11 及以下，系统把蓝牙扫描与位置权限绑定在一起。应用仅对这些版本声明 ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION，且只在你打开蓝牙发现或接收页时于应用内询问一次。你可以拒绝，拒绝后仍能向已配对的设备发送。应用从不读取、不存储、不上传任何位置值。

前台服务与通知。 FOREGROUND_SERVICE 与 FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK 用于让音乐在后台继续播放；POST_NOTIFICATIONS 用于在锁屏与通知栏显示播放控制。

诊断信息

由于应用通过 Google Play 分发，Google 会在系统层面收集应用的质量数据，也就是 Android vitals：崩溃次数与堆栈、无响应（ANR）事件、启动耗时、耗电与内存占用、权限授予与拒绝的情况，以及发生这些问题时的应用版本、Android 版本、设备型号与国家/地区。这些数据由 Google Play 直接收集，不需要应用内嵌任何代码，也不经过我们。它的用途限于评估与改进应用质量，我们只能通过 Play 管理中心看到聚合后的结果，看不到具体某个用户的记录。

这部分收集由 Google 作为独立的控制者进行，受 Google 自身隐私政策约束。你可以在设备的「设置 → Google → 使用情况和诊断信息」中关闭共享；关闭后 Google 不会再从你这台设备收集这些数据。

信息的共享

我们不出售、不出租、不交换你的信息，也不为广告或跨应用追踪而共享。

蓝牙传歌。 应用会打开 Android 系统的分享面板，接收方应用由你选择。文件交给对方之后，其保存、复制与再分发由该应用的政策约束，不在本政策范围内。

保留与删除

界面显示与后台播放所需的播放状态只存在于内存中，退出应用后释放。

我們是誰

NielloPlayer（下稱「本應用程式」）是一款 Android 平台的離線音樂播放器，由 NielloPlayer 的獨立開發者提供。

曲庫與播放狀態。 為了播放音樂，應用程式會讀取你裝置上儲存的音訊檔案。曲庫索引、播放佇列、播放位置、音質與等化器設定，以及歌曲與歌詞的對應關係，保存在應用程式私有目錄中，以便下次開啟時恢復你先前的狀態。這些資訊留在你的裝置上，不會傳送給我們。

應用程式使用的權限

每一項權限都只在對應功能被使用時才起作用，我們不會在你還沒用到該功能時提前索要，也不會因為你拒絕某項選用權限而限制基本播放功能。

音訊檔案。 Android 13 及以上使用 READ_MEDIA_AUDIO，Android 10 至 12 使用 READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE，用於掃描並播放你的音樂。WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE 僅對 Android 10 及以下宣告，用於保留你選定的資料夾。你可以在系統設定中隨時收回這些權限，收回後應用程式只顯示已經索引過的內容。

網路。 INTERNET 用於四件事：在本機提供一個區域網路網頁（網頁傳歌）、在同一區域網路內發現對端裝置、在你選擇打賞時與 Google Play 通訊，以及載入本政策的線上頁面。應用程式不營運任何自有伺服器，也不上傳你的曲庫、歌詞或設定。

Wi-Fi 與熱點狀態。 ACCESS_WIFI_STATE 與 CHANGE_WIFI_STATE 用於讀取 Wi-Fi 是否已連線、熱點是否已開啟，以便告訴你該存取哪個位址、該給什麼提示。部分 Android 版本上，系統要求 CHANGE_WIFI_STATE 才能查詢熱點狀態，這只是讀取的前提。應用程式從不開啟或關閉 Wi-Fi 與熱點，只重複使用你自己在系統設定裡開啟的那個，也不具備把行動數據分享給其它裝置的能力。

藍牙。 BLUETOOTH_CONNECT 用於耳機播放（A2DP）與列出已配對裝置。BLUETOOTH_SCAN 已向系統宣告不用於推斷位置（neverForLocation），用於在應用程式內發現附近的傳送目標。BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE 用於在你開啟接收頁期間，讓對端裝置能夠發現本機——該狀態最長持續 300 秒，到期後由系統自動恢復為無法被發現，應用程式無法延長。Android 11 及以下沒有上述三項細分權限，對應的能力由安裝時授予的 BLUETOOTH 與 BLUETOOTH_ADMIN 涵蓋，應用程式僅用於藍牙耳機播放與傳歌。

Android 11 及以下，系統把藍牙掃描與位置權限綁在一起。應用程式僅對這些版本宣告 ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION，且只在你開啟藍牙發現或接收頁時於應用程式內詢問一次。你可以拒絕，拒絕後仍能向已配對的裝置傳送。應用程式從不讀取、不儲存、不上傳任何位置值。

前景服務與通知。 FOREGROUND_SERVICE 與 FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK 用於讓音樂在背景繼續播放；POST_NOTIFICATIONS 用於在鎖定畫面與通知欄顯示播放控制。

結帳。 com.android.vending.BILLING 是 Google Play 提供選用打賞所必需的權限。

Hvem vi er

NielloPlayer («appen») er en offline-musikafspiller til Android, udbudt af den uafhængige udvikler bag NielloPlayer.

Vi er dataansvarlig for den behandling, der beskrives her. Du når os på den e-mailadresse, der står under «Udviklerens kontaktoplysninger» på Google Play-siden; alle anmodninger om denne politik eller dine oplysninger kan sendes dertil.

Hvad politikken dækker

Politikken beskriver, hvilke oplysninger appen behandler på din enhed, til hvilke formål, og hvilke valg du har. Den gælder Android-versionen.

Politikken findes kun online; der gemmes ingen kopi i appen. Det, du åbner i appen, er altid den gældende version, aldrig en kopi der er blevet forældet sammen med installationspakken.

Den dækker ikke det, der sker, efter du har givet en fil til en anden app. Fra det øjeblik gælder modtagerappens politik – se «Deling af oplysninger».

Hvad vi ikke beder om

Appen har ingen konto og kræver hverken tilmelding eller login. Vi beder ikke om dit navn, din e-mailadresse, dit telefonnummer eller din postadresse, og vi indsamler ikke kontakter, sms- og opkaldshistorik, billeder og videoer, kalender, placering eller annonce-ID.

Der er ingen reklamer i appen, og der er ikke integreret tredjeparts-SDK til analyse, socialt login eller adfærdssporing. Vi laver ingen brugerprofiler, giver ingen målrettede anbefalinger ud fra din adfærd og træffer ingen automatiserede afgørelser.

Oplysninger appen behandler på din enhed

Bibliotek og afspilningstilstand. For at afspille musik læser appen lydfiler på din enhed. Biblioteksindeks, afspilningskø, position, lyd- og equalizerindstillinger samt koblingen mellem sange og tekster gemmes i appens private lager, så din tidligere tilstand gendannes næste gang. Oplysningerne bliver på din enhed og sendes ikke til os.

Mapper du vælger. Mapper uden for systemets mediebibliotek – for eksempel en musikmappe på et SD-kort – nås kun via Androids systemfilvælger. Appen kan kun læse den mappe, du selv har valgt, ingen andre steder, og anmoder ikke om adgang til alle filer. Tekstfiler, du selv tilføjer, godkendes via samme vælger.

Modtagelsesmappe. Filer, der modtages via Bluetooth, lokalnet eller websiden, skrives til den mappe, du vælger under Indstillinger → Bibliotek → Modtagelsesmappe, som standard Music/NielloPlayer i enhedens delte lager. Indholdet der administrerer du selv – se «Deling af oplysninger».

Tilladelser appen bruger

Hver tilladelse virker kun, når den tilhørende funktion bruges. Vi beder ikke på forhånd om noget, du endnu ikke bruger, og et nej til en valgfri tilladelse fjerner aldrig den grundlæggende afspilning.

Lydfiler. Android 13 og nyere bruger READ_MEDIA_AUDIO; Android 10 til 12 bruger READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE. Begge bruges til at scanne og afspille din musik. WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE erklæres kun for Android 10 og ældre for at huske den valgte mappe. Du kan til enhver tid trække tilladelserne tilbage i systemindstillingerne; derefter viser appen kun det, der allerede er indekseret.

Netværk. INTERNET bruges til fire ting: at være vært for en lille lokal webside til sangoverførsel, at finde den anden enhed på samme lokalnet, at kommunikere med Google Play, når du vælger at give en drikkepenge, og at indlæse websiden for denne politik. Appen driver ingen egne servere og uploader ikke dit bibliotek, dine tekster eller indstillinger.

Wi-Fi- og hotspotstatus. ACCESS_WIFI_STATE og CHANGE_WIFI_STATE bruges til at læse, om Wi-Fi er forbundet, og om hotspot er aktiv, så appen kan fortælle, hvilken adresse du skal åbne, og hvilket hint du har brug for. I nogle Android-versioner kræver systemet CHANGE_WIFI_STATE også for at spørge til hotspotstatus; der er den kun en forudsætning for at læse. Appen tænder eller slukker aldrig Wi-Fi eller hotspot, genbruger kun det, du selv har aktiveret i systemindstillingerne, og kan ikke dele mobildata med andre enheder.

Bluetooth. BLUETOOTH_CONNECT bruges til afspilning i hovedtelefoner (A2DP) og til at vise parrede enheder. BLUETOOTH_SCAN er erklæret over for systemet som ikke brugt til at udlede placering (neverForLocation) og bruges til at finde nærliggende overførselsmål i appen. BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE gør det muligt for den anden enhed at finde denne, mens du har modtagelsessiden åben – i højst 300 sekunder, hvorefter systemet vender tilbage til ikke-synlig, og appen kan ikke forlænge det. Android 11 og ældre har ikke disse opdelte tilladelser; de tilsvarende evner kommer fra BLUETOOTH og BLUETOOTH_ADMIN, der gives ved installation, og appen bruger dem kun til Bluetooth-hovedtelefonafspilning og sangoverførsel.

I Android 11 og ældre knytter systemet Bluetooth-scanning til placeringstilladelse. Appen erklærer ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION kun for disse versioner og spørger én gang i appen, kun når du åbner Bluetooth-søgning eller modtagelsessiden. Du må gerne afslå; derefter kan du stadig sende til allerede parrede enheder. Appen læser, gemmer og sender aldrig nogen placeringsværdi.

Forgrundstjeneste og notifikationer. FOREGROUND_SERVICE og FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK holder musikken kørende i baggrunden; POST_NOTIFICATIONS viser afspilningskontroller på låseskærmen og i notifikationspanelet.

Den eneste lokale registrering omkring en drikkepenge er en markering «der er en drikkepenge, vi endnu ikke har takket dig personligt for», som bruges til at vise den rigtige tak næste gang, du åbner drikkepengesiden. Android 11 이하에서 시스템은 Bluetooth 검색과 위치 권한을 묶어 둡니다. 앱은 해당 버전에만 ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION을 선언하고, Bluetooth 검색이나 수신 페이지를 열 때 앱 안에서 한 번만 묻습니다. 거부해도 되며, 거부 후에도 페어링된 기기로는 전송할 수 있습니다. 앱은 위치 값을 읽거나 저장하거나 전송하지 않습니다.

Hvem vi er

NielloPlayer («appen») er en frakoblet musikkavspiller for Android, levert av den uavhengige utvikleren bak NielloPlayer.

Vi er behandlingsansvarlig for behandlingen som beskrives her. Du når oss på e-postadressen som står under «Utviklerens kontaktinformasjon» på Google Play-siden; alle forespørsler om denne erklæringen eller informasjonen din kan sendes dit.

Hva erklæringen dekker

Erklæringen finnes bare på nettet; ingen kopi lagres i appen. Det du åpner i appen er alltid gjeldende versjon, aldri en kopi som er blitt gammel sammen med installasjonspakken.

Den dekker ikke det som skjer etter at du har gitt en fil til en annen app. Fra da av gjelder mottakerappens erklæring – se «Deling av informasjon».

Hva vi ikke ber om

Appen har ingen konto og krever verken registrering eller innlogging. Vi ber ikke om navn, e-postadresse, telefonnummer eller postadresse, og vi samler ikke inn kontakter, SMS- og anropslogg, bilder og videoer, kalender, posisjon eller annonse-ID.
